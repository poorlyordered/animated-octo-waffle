# Quickstart: M31 Opportunity Handoff Retry Controls

1. Open the Opportunity operating layer.
2. Record and approve an Opportunity decision.
3. Create queued work and prepare a worker handoff.
4. Confirm failed handoff details are visible when the handoff fails.
5. Select Schedule handoff retry.
6. Reschedule the retry and apply a delay policy option.
7. Cancel the retry.
8. Confirm all status messages state that no worker was dispatched, claimed, executed, or called external services.
