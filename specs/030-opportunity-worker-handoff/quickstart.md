# Quickstart: M30 Opportunity Worker Handoff

1. Open the Opportunity operating layer.
2. Record a decision from an Opportunity recommendation.
3. Approve the proposed Opportunity decision.
4. Create queued work.
5. Confirm Opportunity queued-work detail shows queue id, status, task intent, expected output, attempts, and no prepared handoff.
6. Select Prepare worker handoff.
7. Confirm the handoff is ready and the browser states that no dispatch, claim, retry, execution, ESI fetch, EVE write, wallet, asset, contract, role, or external-service mutation occurred.
