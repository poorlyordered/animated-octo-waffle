# Quickstart: M47 Operations Health Surface

1. Open the command center.
2. Confirm the Operations Health surface renders.
3. Confirm command API, ingestion, retry, worker readiness, and warnings sections are visible.
4. Confirm worker readiness shows only `configured`, `fallback`, or `missing` state.
5. Confirm the surface has no execution controls.
6. Confirm no browser text includes secret values, token material, connection strings, cookies, raw ESI payloads, or dispatch targets.
7. Run the validation commands from the M47 plan.
