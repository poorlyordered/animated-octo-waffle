# Quickstart: M48 Live Read Consent Expansion

1. Open the ESI token vault surface.
2. Confirm Numbers, People, and Opportunity read-sync domains are listed.
3. Confirm missing scopes are shown when the vault lacks a domain's scopes.
4. Prepare a People or Opportunity read sync from an active vault fixture.
5. Confirm the response is queued and says no ESI data was fetched and no worker was dispatched.
6. Confirm the ESI sync worker does not run People or Opportunity requests in this slice.
7. Run the validation commands from the M48 plan.
